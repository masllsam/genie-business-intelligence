## Executive Summary
This proposal outlines the deployment of autonomous AI agent infrastructure designed for high-availability enterprise workloads, continuous execution, and multi-tenant isolation.

## Key Objectives
- **Zero-Trust Multi-Tenancy**: Complete isolation across database rows, workspaces, and encryption salts.
- **Resilient AI Routing**: Multi-provider fallback cascade spanning OpenRouter, local offline Ollama inference, and Google Cloud endpoints.
- **Autonomous Tool Execution**: Drop-in execution for website generation, secure document compilation, and financial modeling.

## Operational Roadmap & Budget
- **Phase 1 (Q1)**: Infrastructure stabilization and telemetry monitoring.
- **Phase 2 (Q2)**: Scalable credit pack provisioning and enterprise compliance rollout.
